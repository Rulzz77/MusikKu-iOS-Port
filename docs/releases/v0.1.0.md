# MusikKu iOS 0.1.0 (Build 1)

**Status:** merged development baseline — not a signed or published app release  
**Main commit:** `fe5b6e6ae9ede39514c381183c6fc7973714b872`  
**Pull request:** [#1](https://github.com/Rulzz77/MusikKu-iOS-Port/pull/1)

## Included

- Native SwiftUI Beranda, Explore, Koleksi, global search and mini-player navigation.
- Local playlists, favorites, playback history, play-count Mix and Smart Playlist rules.
- AVPlayer queue, repeat/shuffle/seek controls, Now Playing and background audio wiring.
- Lyrics providers, on-demand translation, and TTML import/export.
- iOS Kotlin Multiplatform InnerTubeX resolver with anonymous Webpage Attestation.
- Audio fallback order: direct AAC MP4, progressive AAC MP4, then HLS for AVPlayer.
- AppIcon asset catalog, Privacy Manifest, bundled GPL-3.0 license and third-party notices.
